National policy at a glance

  • Air Act, 1981: the base law; CPCB and state boards set and enforce emission limits.
  • NCAP, 2019: the National Clean Air Programme covers 130+ cities that miss the standards, now targeting up to 40% lower PM10 by 2025-26 against 2017 levels, with funding tied to results.
  • CAQM Act, 2021: created the Commission for Air Quality Management for Delhi-NCR, which runs GRAP.
  • BS-VI, 2020: India jumped from BS-IV to BS-VI vehicle and fuel norms nationwide.
